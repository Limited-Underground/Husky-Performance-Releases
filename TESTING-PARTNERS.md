## Useful feedback

- Husky version and Windows version.
- What you were trying to do and steps to reproduce the issue.
- Expected behavior and what actually happened.
- Whether it happens consistently.
- Log format or export type involved, when relevant.
- A screenshot or small fictional example if helpful.

We address bugs as they are reported and continue improvement through ongoing testing. Written feedback is welcome; no meeting or phone call is required.

## Protect your information

Review screenshots, exports, support packages and feedback files before sharing them. Remove customer and vehicle information, credentials, keys and private business records. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Keep backups and original logs.
